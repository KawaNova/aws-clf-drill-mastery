Ready to ace the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) exam on your first attempt?

Whether you are an aspiring cloud engineer, developer, IT manager, or business professional, foundational AWS knowledge is the single most valuable credential in modern technology. However, memorizing dozens of services—EC2, S3, RDS, DynamoDB, Lambda, VPC, IAM—and understanding pricing tiers and shared responsibility models can feel overwhelming.

"AWS Drill - CLF-C02 Exam Prep" is designed specifically to help you bridge the gap between abstract theory and real-world exam questions. Featuring 30 high-yield questions aligned with the latest CLF-C02 syllabus, ASCII architecture diagrams, in-depth option explanations, "Exam Tips", and an interactive Architecture Diagram Simulator, this app equips you with everything you need to succeed.

### Key Features:

1. **30 High-Yield Exam Questions Across 4 Core Domains**
   Fully updated for the latest CLF-C02 syllabus:
   - **Cloud Concepts**: Agility, elasticity, high availability, Well-Architected Framework, economies of scale.
   - **Security and Compliance**: Shared responsibility model, IAM, Security Groups vs NACLs, AWS Shield & WAF, KMS, compliance artifacts.
   - **Technology & Cloud Services**: Compute (EC2, Lambda, ECS), Storage (S3, EBS, EFS), Databases (RDS, Aurora, DynamoDB), Networking (VPC, CloudFront, Route 53).
   - **Billing, Pricing & Support**: AWS Organizations, Cost Explorer, AWS Budgets, Savings Plans, and Support Plan tiers.

2. **Visual ASCII Architecture Diagrams & Exam Tips**
   Complex network topologies and service relationships are illustrated with clean, terminal-style ASCII diagrams. Every question comes with a high-impact "Exam Tip" highlighting common pitfalls and keywords that frequently appear on the actual exam.

3. **Interactive AWS Architecture Diagram Simulator**
   Explore standard enterprise cloud designs directly on your mobile device:
   - Multi-Tier High Availability Web System
   - Serverless Microservices API
   - Secure Multi-VPC Architecture
   Tap individual components (ALB, Auto Scaling EC2, Multi-AZ Aurora, S3) to inspect their roles, capabilities, and key exam concepts.

4. **Practical AWS Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet**
   Quickly look up essential AWS services with operational summaries and representative AWS CLI commands (`aws ec2 describe-instances`, `aws s3 sync`, `aws lambda invoke`). Perfect for rapid review before your exam.

5. **Built for Focused, Frictionless Study**
   - **Weak Area Review**: Filter and re-test only the questions you got wrong.
   - **Random 10-Question Drill**: Ideal for 5-minute study sessions during your commute.
   - **Bilingual Support (EN / JA)**: Toggle between English and Japanese instantly to prepare for exams in either language.
   - **100% Offline & Ad-Free**: Study anywhere with zero distractions and zero latency.
